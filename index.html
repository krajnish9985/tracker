Export or import your full database (all courses, checkmarks, study notes) as a simple JSON file.

How to open on Phone & Tablet (GitHub Pages):
  1. Go to github.com and click New Repository (name it learneer).
  2. Click Upload files and drop your index.html file there.
  3. Go to Settings → Pages (in the left sidebar).
  4. Under Source, choose Deploy from a branch and select main. Click Save.
  5. In 1 minute, GitHub gives you a free HTTPS link:
    https://your-username.github.io/learneer

Open that link on your phone, tablet, and laptop! Combined with your Sync Key, all devices stay in sync!
